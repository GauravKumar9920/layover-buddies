Duotone (A)Real photos → grayscale → mapped to ink + terracotta (or ink + sea). Paper grain on top. Used full-bleed for mood and section breaks.
Specimen (B)Full-colour photos held in bordered map-cards with a pin, a mono caption, and a coordinate. Reads as samples pinned to a chart.
Faces stay realGuides and street-food moments never go duotone — warmth is the pitch. Treatment is reserved for place, not people.
