Detour · Logo System · Draft v2

The Detour Line + Flight-Tail wordmark

Your two picks, developed and merged into one identity. Concept 01 (the route-into-pin mark) becomes the icon & app/favicon. Concept 05 (the flight-tail wordmark with the italic-serif ou) becomes the typographic signature. The dashed terracotta route line is the shared DNA that ties them together. Still drafts — once you approve the direction, this whole sheet goes to Claude Design for production polish (kerning, true vector export, real favicon files).

The wordmark — “Flight Tail”

Concept 05, refined. Purely typographic — Bricolage Grotesque with the ou set in Instrument Serif italic, and a dashed flight arc that lifts off into a plane. Editorial, magazine energy. Use it standalone in headers and footers where the mark would feel redundant.
