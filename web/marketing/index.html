Why Mumbai first

Not one city — seven contradictions on one peninsula.

Every block is a different city. That's why it stays in your head long after the flight.

01 · Geography

Sea & skyline

The Marine Drive crescent meeting the glass towers of the Worli Sea Link.

02 · Tempo

Chaos & calm

A rush-hour local at CSMT — then Banganga Tank, quiet enough to hear the temple bells.

03 · Time

Old & new

Wooden Portuguese cottages in Khotachiwadi, in the shadow of BKC's high-rises.

04 · Plate

Street & lounges

Midnight street food on Mohammed Ali Road; speakeasy lounges in Bandra.

05 · Stone

Gothic & docks

Victorian stone at CSMT, then the salt-crusted Sassoon Docks.

06 · Spirit

Grit & gold

A mango sold like a business deal at Crawford; Malabar Hill's mansions behind the banyans.

07 · Culture

Stars & shrines

Bollywood marquees in Bandra; the causeway to Haji Ali, out in the sea.

Trust & safety

Privacy Policy

At Detour, we hold your trust and safety above all else. Here is how we handle your information:

1. Information We Collect: We only collect information that you explicitly share with us: your name, email, layover date, flight times, and preferences. We do not use trackers or share data with third parties.

2. How We Use It: Your details are used solely to match you with a verified student buddy. Once your detour is complete, your flight and terminal details are deleted from our active database.

3. Security: Student buddies undergo strict background verification. Your contact information is only shared with your assigned buddy 24 hours prior to landing.

Questions? Reach us at admin@detourtrips.com.

How we operate

Terms of Service

Welcome to Detour! By requesting a detour, you agree to the following terms:

1. Student Buddies: Detour matches travellers with local university students. While we verify student status and run background checks, buddies are independent local guides and not professional commercial tour agencies.

2. Fixed Pricing: We guarantee clear, transparent, upfront rates. Your buddy will never take commissions, suggest forced shopping, or request unexpected cash surcharges.

3. Safety & Buffer Guarantee: Buddies are trained to design routes that keep you inside the airport buffer time. Detour is not liable for delayed flights or missed departures due to external traffic anomalies, but we prioritize getting you back safely and on time.
