Why Mumbai first

Mumbai isn't one city. It's seven contradictions stacked on one peninsula.

That's exactly why it stays in your head long after the flight. Every block is a different city — slide or swipe to explore them all.

01 · Geography

Sea & skyline.

The refreshing Arabian Sea breeze along the Marine Drive crescent meeting the towering glass skyline of the Worli Sea Link coastline.

02 · Tempo

Chaos & calm.

A local train at CSMT in rush hour will rearrange your idea of crowded. Twenty minutes away, Banganga Tank is so quiet you can hear the temple bells across the water.

03 · Time

Old & new.

Centuries-old, beautifully preserved wooden Portuguese cottages in Khotachiwadi standing in the shadow of the sprawling financial high-rises of BKC.

04 · Plate

Street & lounges.

Piping hot, spicy midnight street food at Mohammed Ali Road contrasting with the high-concept speakeasy lounges of Colaba and Bandra.

05 · Stone

Gothic & docks.

The majestic Victorian stone carvings of Crawford and CSMT standing alongside the raw, salt-crusted maritime history of the Sassoon Docks.

06 · Spirit

Grit & gold.

At Crawford Market a wholesaler will sell you a single mango like it's a business deal. Ten minutes up the hill, Malabar's old mansions sit behind banyan trees pretending the city isn't there.

07 · Culture

Stars & shrines.

The shimmering Bollywood movie marquees of Bandra existing alongside the ancient spiritual path of Haji Ali Mosque extending into the Arabian Sea.

How we travel

We care how we move through a city. Not just where we go.

Mumbai is generous, but a few corners need care. We don't sell poverty as spectacle, and we don't cut corners on safety. Here's the line we hold.

Dignity

Community-led, never "slum sightseeing"

Where we visit working neighbourhoods, it's with community-run guides, focused on craft and enterprise — never camera-pointing tours.

Safety

Known venues, planned transport

Every evening detour ends with a vetted cab. We never put a solo traveller on a late-night local train.

Honesty

Fixed price, no surprises

The number you see is the number you pay. No surge, no commission detours, no end-of-day surprise bill.

Respect

The city's terms, not ours

Prayer times, market days, dress at sacred sites, photography etiquette — your buddy knows, so you never have to guess.

Trust & safety

Privacy Policy

At Detour, we hold your trust and safety above all else. Here is how we handle your information:

1. Information We Collect: We only collect information that you explicitly share with us: your name, email, layover date, flight times, and preferences. We do not use trackers or share data with third parties.

2. How We Use It: Your details are used solely to match you with a verified student buddy at the target airport. Once your detour is complete, your flight and terminal details are deleted from our active database.

3. Security: Student buddies undergo strict background verification. Your contact information is only shared with your assigned buddy 24 hours prior to landing.

If you have any questions, reach out to us at admin@detourtrips.com.

How we operate

Terms of Service

Welcome to Detour! By requesting a detour, you agree to the following terms:

1. Student Buddies: Detour matches travellers with local university students. While we verify student status and run background checks, buddies are independent local guides and not professional commercial tour agencies.

2. Fixed Pricing: We guarantee clear, transparent, upfront rates. Your buddy will never take commissions, suggest forced shopping, or request unexpected cash surcharges.

3. Safety & Buffer Guarantee: Buddies are trained to design routes that keep you inside the airport buffer time. Detour is not liable for delayed flights or missed departures due to external traffic anomalies, but we prioritize getting you back safely and on time.
