# Review-Ask — WhatsApp (Same Day)

**When:** the day of / day after a completed detour, while the glow is fresh. This is the single highest-leverage message in the whole pipeline — every founding traveler is a potential review + photo + Reel.

**Channel:** WhatsApp (where the conversation already lives). Keep it warm and human, never templated-sounding.

---

## Message 1 — the thank-you + soft ask (send same day)

Hey [name]! 🙏 It was so good having you in Mumbai today — [specific detail: "your reaction to the vada pav was perfect" / "hope the rest of the flight was smooth"]. [Buddy name] said you were brilliant company.

One tiny favour, only if you're up for it: we're a brand-new team and honest reviews from our first travellers are everything to us. Would you mind leaving a quick line about your detour? Takes 30 seconds:
👉 [Google review link]
👉 [Trustpilot link]

And if you snapped any photos you'd be happy for us to share, send them my way — we'd love to (with credit, only if you're cool with it). Safe travels home! ✈️

---

## Message 2 — gentle nudge (only if no response after ~2 days, send once)

Hey [name], no pressure at all — just floating this back up in case it got buried. Even one line means a lot to a team this small. 🙏 [review link] · Hope you're home safe!

---

## Variations by sentiment
- **They were delighted in person:** ask directly, they'll say yes. Add: "If you'd ever be up for a quick 20-sec video clip for our Instagram, even better — but a written line is plenty."
- **They were quiet/neutral:** skip the review ask; instead ask for *feedback* ("anything we could've done better? we're learning fast"). Protects your rating and improves the product.
- **Something went wrong:** no review ask at all. Apologise, fix it, learn. One bad early review outweighs ten good ones.

## Notes
- Always credit photos and get explicit OK before posting anyone's face.
- Log outcome (reviewed? photo? reel?) in the Notion metrics log.
- The flywheel: review + photo + one Reel ("[Name] had 9 hours in Mumbai. Here's what happened.") per completed trip.
