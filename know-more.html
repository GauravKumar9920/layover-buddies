The startup story behind LayoverBuddies

We turn Mumbai layovers into guided local experiences.

The idea is simple: international travelers with long layovers can explore the city with a trained local student guide instead of waiting at the airport. Travelers get a personalized plan. Students get flexible earning while studying. The platform ensures quality, safety, and fair structure for everyone.

The Model

One city. Two user groups. One platform.

This startup solves two real problems at once: idle layovers for travelers and inflexible part-time options for students.

Traveler Problem

International travelers with 5-12 hour layovers usually stay stuck at the airport, even when the city is nearby.

Student Opportunity

Mumbai students from strong colleges can earn while studying by sharing local culture, routes, food, and stories.

Platform Role

We match travelers and guides, structure pricing transparently, and maintain quality through interview and ratings.

For Travelers

How a layover experience works

STEP 01

Share Flight Window

Tell us arrival and departure times. We confirm if a safe city window exists.

STEP 02

Get Personalized Plan

You receive a route based on your layover duration, interests, and traffic realities.

STEP 03

Explore with a Student Guide

Your local buddy guides transport, local stops, food picks, and timing.

STEP 04

Return with Buffer Time

The route always includes airport return margin so you can fly stress-free.

Guide Economy

Earn with flexibility while studying

Students can take tours during free slots and build real-world communication and city leadership skills.

Pricing Model

Transparent structure, no hidden markup

Final trip estimates are shared before confirmation. Public pricing is currently high-level while we optimize packages.

Guide Service Fee

Compensation for your student guide's hosted time and local planning support.

Experience Expenses

Food, transit, and tickets paid directly at actual rates during the tour.

Variable Ops Cost

Time-window complexity, route distance, and traffic risk influence planning effort.

Platform Charge

Supports matching, quality control, traveler support, and guide operations.

Quality and Safety

How we keep the experience reliable

Guide Screening

Applications, interviews, and local-fit checks are used before guides go live.

Rating-Based Quality

Traveler feedback impacts guide ranking and visibility, pushing quality upward over time.

Return-Time Discipline

Routes are planned around departure schedules with buffer margins, not just sightseeing speed.

Why Mumbai, Why Students

Local depth matters more than generic tours

Mumbai has dense culture, food variety, and neighborhood contrasts that are hard to navigate quickly without local context. Students who live this city daily can give travelers a richer and more human experience than checklist tourism.

For students, this model creates flexible earning with confidence-building exposure. For travelers, it creates trust, speed, and authenticity during a short layover window.
