# Creator Pitch — Free Detour for a Real BOM Layover

**Targets:** travel YouTubers / Instagram & TikTok creators / newsletter writers who (a) have an engaged travel audience and (b) realistically transit Mumbai. Prioritise mid-size creators (10k–200k) — better reply rates and engagement than mega-accounts.

**Angle:** "You've got a layover coming — let us turn it into content for you, free." Give, don't ask.

**Send via:** the channel they actually read (often Instagram DM or their listed business email).

---

## Instagram DM (short)

Hey [name] — love your [specific video/post]. Quick one: do you ever transit through Mumbai? We're Detour — we pair travellers on long BOM layovers with a verified local student who plans the day around their flight and meets them at arrivals. It's free in early access.

If you've got a Mumbai layover coming up, we'd love to host you on one — no strings, no script. Just think it'd make a great "what to do on a layover" piece for your audience. Worth a chat?

---

## Email version

Subject: A free Mumbai layover detour for [channel]?

Hi [First name],

I love what you do on [channel] — [one specific, genuine detail].

I run Detour (detourtrips.com). We turn long Mumbai layovers into real city time: a verified local college student plans a flight-safe route, meets the traveller at arrivals, and gets them back with a buffer. Free while we're in early access.

If you have a BOM layover on the horizon — or want to route one in — I'd love to host you on a detour, on us. No content obligation and no script: if it's good, you'll want to share it; if it's not, we'll have failed and that's on us. Either way you get a real day in Mumbai instead of a transit lounge.

The honest pitch: we're new, we're proud of this, and a creator's real layover is exactly the kind of story we're built for.

Up for it? Tell me your routing and I'll sort a buddy.

Gaurav · Founder, Detour (Mumbai)
admin@detourtrips.com · instagram.com/detour_trips

---

## Notes
- Lead with a *specific* compliment — proof you actually watch/read them.
- "No content obligation" disarms them and, paradoxically, makes them more likely to post.
- Make the yes one step: "tell me your routing and I'll sort a buddy."
- Track every pitch in the Notion Outreach CRM (status: to-pitch → pitched → replied).
