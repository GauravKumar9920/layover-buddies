# Research & Market Analysis

## Purpose
Store all market research, competitor analysis, user interviews, surveys, and validation data. Use this folder to document opportunities, threats, and customer insights.

## Contents
- Competitive landscape analysis (similar platforms, pricing, features)
- Market size & opportunity assessment
- User research & persona development
- Customer interviews & feedback summaries
- Travel industry trends & statistics
- Student guide market opportunity in Mumbai
- Pricing benchmarking across competitors
- Regulatory environment (tourism, employment laws)

## Research Templates
- Competitor Analysis Template
- User Interview Notes Template
- Survey Results Summary
- Market Opportunity Assessment
- SWOT Analysis

## Tools Recommended
- **Surveys**: Google Forms, Typeform, SurveyMonkey
- **Interview Recording**: Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai
- **Competitive Analysis**: Airtable, Google Sheets
- **Documentation**: Notion, Confluence

## Key Questions to Answer
1. Who are our primary competitors?
2. What is our unique value proposition?
3. What are customers willing to pay?
4. What geographies should we target first?
5. What are the regulatory constraints?
6. What marketing channels reach our audience?
