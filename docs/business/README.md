# Business Documents

## Purpose
Store all business planning materials, financial projections, pitch decks, strategic plans, and investor documentation. This folder helps organize the business strategy and operational planning.

## Contents
- Business Plan (executive summary, strategy, go-to-market)
- Financial Projections (revenue model, unit economics, 3-5 year forecast)
- Pitch Deck (investor presentation)
- Market Opportunity Summary
- Customer Acquisition Strategy
- Pricing Strategy & Justification
- Revenue Model (commission structure, subscription options)
- Break-even Analysis
- Runway Calculation
- Milestone Timeline

## Business Model Options
1. **Commission-Based**: Take 15-25% cut from each booking
2. **Subscription**: Monthly subscription for guides to list profiles
3. **Freemium**: Free bookings with optional paid features
4. **Hybrid**: Monthly subscription + reduced commission

## Key Metrics to Track
- Monthly Active Users (travelers & guides)
- Booking Volume & Growth Rate
- Average Booking Value (ABV)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Guide Satisfaction & Retention
- Conversion Rate (search to booking)
- Refund/Cancellation Rate

## Go-to-Market Strategy
1. **Phase 1**: Beta launch in Mumbai with 50-100 guides
2. **Phase 2**: Marketing push targeting international travelers
3. **Phase 3**: Expand to other Indian cities (Delhi, Bangalore, Goa)
4. **Phase 4**: International expansion
