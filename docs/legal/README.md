# Legal & Compliance Documents

## Purpose
Store all legal agreements, compliance documents, and regulatory documentation. Ensure data privacy, user protection, and legal alignment with Indian law and international requirements.

## Contents
- Terms of Service (travelers & guides)
- Privacy Policy (GDPR, India Privacy Standards)
- User Agreement & Liability Waivers
- Guide Contractor Agreement
- Payment Terms & Refund Policy
- Copyright & IP Licensing
- Compliance Checklist (India-specific)
- Insurance Documentation
- Tax & Licensing Registrations

## India-Specific Considerations
- **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**: User dispute resolution
- **Information Technology Act, 2000**: Data protection & cyber security
- **Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA)**: International payment handling
- **GST Compliance**: Tax registration & invoicing
- **Employment Classification**: Are guides contractors or employees?
