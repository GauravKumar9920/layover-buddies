Got Questions?

What if my incoming flight is delayed?

We monitor flight statuses in real-time. Your buddy will wait for you or adjust the itinerary on the fly. You only pay for the time you actually spend touring.

What do I do with my heavy luggage?

Mumbai Airport has a secure left-luggage cloakroom. Your buddy will meet you at arrivals and escort you directly there so your city run stays easy.

How does the pricing work?

You pay a flat hourly or half-day fee online to reserve your buddy. During the tour, you directly pay transportation, food, and tickets with no hidden markup.
