Juhu Beach.

Juhu is Mumbai’s broad, democratic evening beach: families, walkers, vendors, street food and sunset gathering on the western edge of the city.

01 · Why make the detour

Why visit Juhu Beach?

Juhu is not the city’s quietest beach; its appeal is atmosphere. Come for the crowd, the food and the long horizon. For layover travellers, its western-suburb location can preserve far more actual exploring time than a trip to South Mumbai.

05 · Local field notes

The details that make it better.

Sea safety

Follow local warnings and avoid entering rough or polluted water.

Food

Choose busy stalls with fast turnover; drink sealed or trusted water.

Crowds

Evenings and weekends can become extremely busy.

Weather

Monsoon changes beach conditions quickly.
