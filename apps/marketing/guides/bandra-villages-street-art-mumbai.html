01 · Why make the detour

Why visit Bandra Villages & Street Art?

The appeal is contrast: old settlement patterns beside contemporary cafés and street art. Ranwar and nearby lanes are lived-in places, not an outdoor museum, so the best walk stays curious without intruding into homes or blocking narrow streets.

02 · A route through the place

Stop 02 · 30 min
