01 · Why make the detour

Why visit Worli Sea Face?

The promenade gives you infrastructure, fishing history and everyday exercise culture in one stretch. It is a good connective stop between western suburbs and South Mumbai, but it is equally valid as the place where you simply sit and stop adding things.

05 · Local field notes

The details that make it better.

Monsoon

Strong waves and spray can make the edge unsafe; respect barricades.

Crossing

Use safe crossings on roads around the promenade.

Photography

Keep distance from working fishing spaces and ask before portraits.

Food

Plan the meal elsewhere in Worli, Dadar or Bandra.
