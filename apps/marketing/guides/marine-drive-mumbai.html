01 · Why make the detour

Why visit Marine Drive?

The promenade is a release valve for the city. Office workers, runners, families and travellers share the same edge of the Arabian Sea. At night the arc of lights earns its Queen’s Necklace nickname, but daylight is better for seeing the Art Deco skyline behind you.

05 · Local field notes

The details that make it better.

Best light

Sunset is dramatic; early morning is calmer and cooler.

Monsoon

Expect spray, strong rain and temporary safety restrictions near rough water.

Food

Chowpatty has more snack options than the central promenade.

Safety

Use marked crossings; the road beside the promenade is fast and busy.
