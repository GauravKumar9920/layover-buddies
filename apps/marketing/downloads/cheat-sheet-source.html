Your Mumbai layover, sorted — on one page.

1 Visa — do this first

You need a visa to leave the terminal. Most travellers use the e-Tourist visa.

  • Apply online ≈ 1 week before you fly
  • Cost ≈ US$25 (varies by nationality/season)
  • Approval usually 24–72h — print the ETA
  • Only use the official indianvisaonline.gov.in
  • Passport valid 6+ months, 2 blank pages

2 Time budget

Your layover length ≠ your city time. Subtract immigration + drive + buffer.

  • <6h: tight — maybe stay near the airport
  • 6–8h: one neighbourhood, done well
  • 10–12h: the greatest hits + a real meal
  • 18h+: a proper day in the city

Golden rule: never touch your airport buffer.

3 Luggage

BOM has a secure left-luggage cloakroom — pay per bag, explore hands-free.

  • Keep on you: passport, ETA printout, phone, cash, meds, claim ticket
  • Stash the heavy roller bag
  • Allow a few minutes each end

5 Safety basics

  • Central areas are busy & fine — risks are petty, not dramatic
  • App cabs (Uber/Ola), esp. after dark
  • Sealed bottled water only
  • Carry cash; know rough prices to avoid markup
  • Solo women: share live location, ask for a female buddy

6 Money & getting around

  • India runs on UPI — but carry cash as a visitor
  • Airport → South Mumbai taxi ≈ ₹500–700
  • Sea Link toll ₹100
  • eSIM/roaming is easier than a local SIM for a few hours
  • Monsoon (Jun–Sep): assume rain

✦ Leave the airport in 6 steps

  • Sort your e-visa about a week before flying
  • Land & clear immigration (biometrics on arrival)
  • Drop bags at the cloakroom
  • Head into the city — buddy meets you at arrivals
  • Eat, wander, sea — keep an eye on the buffer
  • Collect bags & back to BOM, comfortably early
