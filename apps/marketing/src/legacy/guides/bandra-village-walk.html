Self-guided walk · Near the airport · About 2 km

Fort, sea and chapel.

Bandra is the easiest part of the old city to reach from the airport, and it’s best on foot: a Portuguese fort, a seafront promenade, a hilltop basilica and lanes painted with film stars.

Updated September 2026· 5 min read· About 1½–2 hours at an easy pace

This short walk strings together three of our near-airport field notes. It starts by the sea, climbs a gentle hill and ends in the old lanes of Bandra, with cafés at every turn.

Before you start

  • Distance: about 2 km, or 1½–2 hours with stops.
  • Best time: early morning or late afternoon; the promenade is lovely towards sunset.
  • Terrain: a gentle climb to the basilica and a flight of steps down. Wear shoes you can slip off at the church.

Open the walk in Google Maps ↗

The route

  1. Bandra Fort. Start at Land’s End, where the Portuguese built a watchtower in 1640 over Mahim Bay and the sea. Read the field note. Map ↗
  2. Bandstand Promenade. Walk the 1.2 km seafront path with the Sea Link on the horizon. Joggers, couples and chai sellers keep it busy from early morning. Map ↗
  3. Mount Mary Basilica. Head uphill to the Basilica of Our Lady of the Mount, a place of pilgrimage for people of many faiths. Follow the customs posted at the entrance. Read the field note. Map ↗
  4. The Mount Mary steps. Walk down the old flight of steps towards the lanes of the village below. Map ↗
  5. Chapel Road. Finish in the lanes around Chapel Road, where street art brightens the walls, including murals from the Bollywood Art Project, started in 2012. These are people’s homes: keep your voice down and ask before photographing anyone. Map ↗

Fitting it into a layover

Bandra is roughly 20–45 minutes from the airport by road, which makes this walk possible on a shorter layover than anything in South Mumbai. With about 6 hours on the ground and no bags to collect, it can work if your flight lands on time; check your window with the layover checker.

In the week after 8 September, the Mount Mary Fair brings very large crowds to the hill. See festivals and events.

Quick answers

How long is the Bandra walk?
About 2 km from Bandra Fort to Chapel Road. With stops, allow 1½ to 2 hours.
Can I walk around Bandra on a short layover?
Often, yes. Bandra is roughly 20–45 minutes from the airport by road, so with about 6 hours on the ground, carry-on bags and an on-time flight, this walk can fit.
Can I visit Mount Mary Basilica as a tourist?
Yes. It welcomes visitors of all faiths. Follow the customs posted at the entrance, dress modestly and keep quiet during services.
