Mumbai airport to the city, the layover way

Once you’re through immigration, the next question is how to cross the city without burning your layover in traffic. Here are the real options from BOM, what each is good for, and how to make sure the ride back never becomes the stressful part.

Updated September 2026· 6 min read· Get this right and the day gets longer

Mumbai is a long, narrow city, and the airport sits in the suburbs, a good way north of the Gateway of India and Marine Drive. The drive south is usually the biggest single block of time in a layover, so choosing how you travel matters more than choosing what to see.

T1 or T2? Know where you land

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport has two terminals. Most international flights use Terminal 2, and Terminal 1 is mainly domestic. If you arrive at one terminal and leave from the other, the airport runs a free shuttle between T1 and T2. Check your onward terminal before you plan the return, because it changes where your driver needs to drop you.

As a feel for road travel: a cab from the airport towards South Mumbai commonly costs ≈ ₹500–₹700 each way and takes 45–75 minutes on an ordinary day, longer at rush hour or in heavy rain. Bandra and Juhu are much closer. Prices move with demand, so treat these as a feel, not a quote.

Skip the touts Inside arrivals you may be offered a ride by someone without a booking. Say no politely and walk to the official app-cab pickup zone or the taxi counter. It’s safer, and the price is clear before you move.

The metro: the traffic-free way south

Mumbai Metro Line 3, the Aqua Line, has stations at both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 and runs underground through the city to Cuffe Parade. Along the way it stops at Siddhivinayak, Worli, Grant Road, Girgaon, Kalbadevi, CSMT, Hutatma Chowk and Churchgate, which puts the Fort heritage district and Marine Drive within a short walk.

Because it avoids the roads, the metro is the most predictable way into South Mumbai when traffic is bad. It works best if you’ve stored your big bags at the airport cloakroom. Check current timings, fares and service updates on the official Mumbai Metro Rail Corporation channels before you build a plan around it.

The ride back is the one that matters

Getting into the city late costs you a few minutes of sightseeing. Getting back late costs you your flight. Plan the return first:

  • Work backwards from your onward check-in, not your departure time. Leave room for security and emigration on the way out.
  • Add slack for rush hour. Evening traffic heading north can be much slower than your morning ride in.
  • Book early. Request your cab before the last stop ends, or head for a metro station with time to spare.
  • Check the terminal. If you leave from a different terminal than you arrived at, tell your driver or allow time for the free shuttle.

Quick answers

What is the fastest way from Mumbai airport to South Mumbai?
When the roads are busy, the Aqua Line metro is usually the most predictable option: it runs underground from both airport terminals to CSMT and Churchgate. Off-peak with luggage, an app cab or airport taxi is simpler. Check current metro timings and fares before you travel.
How much is a taxi from Mumbai airport to the city?
As a rough feel, a cab from the airport towards South Mumbai is about ₹500–₹700 each way, and less to Bandra or Juhu. App-cab prices rise at busy times, and official airport taxis can cost more. Always confirm the fare in the app or at the counter before you leave.
Can I take the metro from Mumbai airport?
Yes. Metro Line 3, the Aqua Line, has stations at Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 and runs south through the city to Cuffe Parade, with stops at CSMT and Churchgate.
How do I get between Terminal 1 and Terminal 2?
The airport runs a free shuttle between T1 and T2. Allow time for it if your onward flight leaves from a different terminal.
