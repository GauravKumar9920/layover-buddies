How it works · Step by step

What a detour looks like.

You’ve landed in a city you’ve never seen, with a flight to catch. Here’s how a Detour works, from your first message to walking back into the terminal with time to spare.

Updated September 2026· 4 min read· Planned backwards from your flight

Detour is a small, founder-hosted service. For our first trips, your host is Gaurav or his co-founder, and every route is planned around one fixed point: your onward flight.

Before you fly

  1. You share your flight times. Arrival, departure and anything that would make the day easier: food, pace, places you’d love to see.
  2. We work out your real window. Immigration on the way out, the drive each way and the time you need back at the airport all come off first. Our layover checker uses the same maths.
  3. You see the plan. Stops, timings and who’s meeting you, before you land. If your window is too tight, we’ll say so.

When you land

  1. We follow your flight. If you land late, your host already knows and the plan adjusts.
  2. Your host meets you at arrivals once you’ve cleared immigration.
  3. Bags first. If you’ve collected luggage, your host walks you to the airport cloakroom so you can explore hands-free. See the luggage guide.

In the city

  1. At your pace. A slow chai counts as much as a monument. We can swap, shorten or skip stops as the day goes.
  2. Someone who knows the city beside you. Which stall, which lane, which road will be quicker at five o’clock.
  3. Shared with home. You can share your live location with someone back home for the whole detour.

Heading back

  1. We leave on time, not late. Your host watches traffic and turns back early if the roads start to slow.
  2. A planned ride. Evening detours end in a vetted cab to the terminal, never a late-night local train.
  3. Back with a buffer. You walk into departures with the time your airline asks for, and a few stories.

If anything changes along the way, our what-if guide explains what happens.

What you pay for

You pay for your own cabs, food and tickets directly, at local prices. Your hosts never take commissions or steer you towards shops. Our money guide has typical costs.

Quick answers

Who is my host on a Detour?
During early access, your host is one of Detour’s founders. Student buddies are planned for January 2027.
What happens if my flight lands late?
We follow your flight, so your host knows about the delay and waits. The route gets shorter or moves closer to the airport; your time back at the airport is never cut.
What do I do with my luggage?
If you’ve collected bags, your host walks you to the airport cloakroom first, so you can explore hands-free.
