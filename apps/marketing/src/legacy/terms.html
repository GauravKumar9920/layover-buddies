5. Bookings & payments

When a Traveller and Guide agree an experience in-app, a booking is created that moves through a defined lifecycle (inquiry → agreement → deposit → balance → trip → settlement). Both sides pay a refundable deposit to confirm commitment; the Traveller pays the remaining balance before the trip. Amounts are shown in Indian Rupees (₹) and are calculated by the Service from the agreed itinerary — you should not rely on any figure quoted outside the app.

Payments are processed by our third-party payment provider (Razorpay). We do not store full card details. By paying, you also agree to the payment provider's terms.

Detour holds trip funds and releases them to the Guide after the experience, less platform fees and applicable taxes. The Guide — not Detour — is responsible for delivering the experience.

6. Cancellations & refunds

Cancellation outcomes depend on who cancels and how close to the trip it happens, and are calculated and disclosed to you in-app before you confirm a cancellation:

  • Traveller cancellations are resolved on a tiered basis by how much notice is given; deposits and balances may be refunded in full, partially, or as credit depending on the tier shown to you.
  • Guide cancellations release the Traveller's funds and may credit the Traveller.
  • Platform- or force-majeure cancellations (events outside either party's control, or a cancellation we initiate) are treated as no-fault: deposits and balances are refunded and neither party is penalised.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible and may take several business days to appear.
